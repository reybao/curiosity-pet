| zhwiki_content (9 shards) | 7.2 GB | 1,561,533 | 157,980 (only articles about the same concepts as Simple English Wikipedia, by Wikidata item) |

Chinese articles outside that set were measured on one shard before deciding: ranked by incoming links they are mostly
counties and districts; ranked by popularity they are elections, politicians, celebrities and TV dramas. Neither suits a
child, so they are left out. Chinese culture topics a child asks about (春节, 长城, 中秋节, 西游记, 孙悟空) are in the matched set.
Chinese text is converted to Simplified with OpenCC; leading coordinates and Wikipedia notices are removed.

Result: `~/pet-data-dev/library/library.db`, 451 MB (opening paragraph about 600 characters, redirect titles as aliases,
SQLite FTS5: English with the Porter stemmer, Chinese as character bigrams). Build time 4 minutes on the M4 with 9 workers,
peak memory of the main process 435 MB.

## Ranking

Rewritten queries are 1 to 4 words. Candidates come from exact title or redirect matches and full-text search; the
library ranks them itself (words in title and opening paragraph, English by whole word and singular, an exact title match,
"孙悟空 (七龙珠)" style titles, how well linked the article is) and requires the first word, usually the subject, to appear.
Its first result is used as is; an empty result means the online providers are tried.
Fixes found while testing: "bee" matched "been" (now whole words), "bees" produced the variant "be", an alias of Basic
English (fixed), "星星 眨眼" returned eye blinking (subject rule), "小猫" did not match 猫 (diminutive dropped).

`evals/library_eval.py`: 26/26 first results as expected; search median 15 ms, max 215 ms (cold cache).

## School poems, character dictionary, riddles (2026-10-24 evening)

Gaps seen in the child's real questions that day: "锄禾日当午下面是什么" (悯农 was not in 唐诗三百首 or 千家诗), riddles she told
the pet ("What has hands but cannot grab things?" went to the library and came back as "Hand"), and character questions.

**School poems.** `data/school_poems.json` lists 113 poems taught in primary school (the curriculum standard's 75 plus
common textbook poems), with title, author and first line only; it is a compiled list, not an official catalogue.
`pet-library school-poems` finds each text in local files: 唐诗三百首 and 千家诗 first (school versions), then chinese-poetry's
全唐诗, 全宋诗, 宋词 and 诗经, then Werneror/Poetry (MIT, 85k+ poems by dynasty; Han, Yuan, Ming, Qing). A text counts only
when the known first line starts a line and the author matches (anonymous sources for 汉乐府 and similar).
A first attempt used Wikisource search and was dropped: it returned a court document for 敕勒歌 and a parody for 七步诗.
Result: 110 of 113 found (唐诗三百首 27, 全唐诗 30, 全宋诗 18, 千家诗 16, Werneror 15, 宋词 3, 诗经 1); 9 of them exist
only in a classical variant (for example 别董大 "十里黄云", 游园不值 "应嫌屐齿"), and the entry says the textbook wording
differs slightly. Not found: 画 (王维), 墨梅 (王冕), 所见 (袁枚). Every resolved text was read through once.

**Character dictionary.** chinese-xinhua `word.json` (MIT): 16,141 characters with pinyin, radical, strokes, traditional
form (only when OpenCC confirms it) and the modern senses (classical quotations removed). Its word list `ci.json` (264k)
was not used: it is a classical dictionary (no 彩虹 or 高兴; 朋友 includes "lover").
Dictionary entries rank first only for questions about a character ("猫字怎么写", "好是什么意思", "森林的森是什么部首").

**Questions answered straight from the child's words.** The model rewrite turned "鹅鹅鹅后面是什么?" into "鹅" and called
"你会背咏鹅吗?" small talk. Poem questions (下一句, 后面是什么, 会背, 这首诗) and character questions now take the line,
title or character from the child's own words and search only the library (nothing leaves the machine, no model call,
about 0 s instead of 2.5 s), and only when the library has it.

**Riddles.** The bank grew from 60 + 60 to 160 + 160: 30 classic Chinese character riddles (一口咬掉牛尾巴 -> 告, 日月同辉
-> 明), 70 more Chinese and 100 more English riddles, all original wording with accepted answers and a hint.
When the child tells a riddle, the pet looks it up in the bank first (similarity on character pairs or content words,
riddle boilerplate such as 什么东西 removed): a classic is answered "这个我知道!是告!我猜对了吗?" without the model; an unknown
one is still guessed by the model. Riddles told without an announcement are recognized by unmistakable forms
("What has ... but ...", "打一个字", "什么东西...越...越", "Who am I?"). Every bank riddle recognizes itself; on that day's
real turns exactly the five riddle turns were flagged.
