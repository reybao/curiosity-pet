# Blind Scoring Sheet

Each question has two answers from two different models, order randomized. Score each item 1 to 5, 5 is best.

Items: **Correct** (factually right), **Clear** (a 7-year-old can follow), **Lang** (answers in the language of the question), **Short** (two or three sentences, fine to read aloud).
