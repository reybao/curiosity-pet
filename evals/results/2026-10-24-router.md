## What changed between the last two runs

The check (`router.py`) only runs on doubtful messages: 4 words or fewer, or ones speaking of someone as 他/它/he/it,
and never while the pet is waiting for an answer. Every route difference between the two runs is a judged message:

- 8 better: 3 adult side remarks now ignored, 5 garbled recognitions now get "I didn't catch that" instead of an answer.
- 1 worse: an adult relaying a division question, which mentions a third person, was judged side talk and ignored.
- 1 left out: the pet asked a different question this time, so the labelled context did not occur.

By intent: side 0/16 to 3/16, garbled 4/22 to 9/22, math 14/15 to 13/15; all others unchanged.

## Cost

- Paired by turn, judged messages that still got an answer started speaking about 2.0 s later (median, 20 turns);
  messages not judged were unchanged (+0.2 s, run-to-run noise; 69 turns).
- The model run varies between runs in wording: "claim" flags (raw model text before the guardrails) went 3 to 7, and
  one greeting got no spoken answer in the router run; neither turn was judged by the check, so these are not caused
  by it. They are tracked as their own problems.

## Decision

Turned on (`"router": true` in settings.json): 80% against 72%, the parent prefers smarter over faster, and the cost is
limited to short or third-person messages. The 32 side or garbled turns still answered are the largest remaining
group, followed by 5 knowledge questions answered without a lookup and 3 poem requests.
