## 2. Reviewed child Q&A library

Seed data (repo): `data/qa_zh.json` 505 entries (357 questions, 110 poem meaning notes, 38 idiom stories) and
`data/qa_en.json` 356 questions. Topics: everyday science and weather, animals and plants, the body and habits, space,
the Earth and everyday things. Drafted by the large model (five parallel drafting passes with fixed rules: grade 1-2
wording, at most 80 Chinese characters or 45 English words, textbook facts only, myths excluded, uncertain science
said as such), merged with duplicate, length, myth and "ask your parents" checks, and spot-read.

Every entry is pending; pending and rejected entries are never served. The parent reviews with
`uv run pet-library qa stats | list | show | review | approve | reject`; the verdicts are stored outside git in
`~/pet-data-dev/library/qa_status.json` and take effect at once (no rebuild, no restart).

Serving: an approved answer ranks above everything else. An exact match (same content words, no close second) is used
in `decide`, skipping the rewrite call. Close candidates (up to three, word overlap with jieba words or English stems,
counted both ways) go to the local model, which picks the one that asks the same thing or none; this runs in `fetch`,
after the "let me look it up" filler, so the filler is not delayed. With no candidate there is no model call.

Leave-one-out on 80 sampled alternative phrasings per language (each phrasing removed from the store, all entries
treated as approved):
