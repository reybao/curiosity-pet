## Speak latency on the current setup

`uv run python evals/latency_eval.py --runs 2` (raw data: `2026-11-17-latency-raw.json`). Eight made-up questions
(Chinese, English, mixed), synthesized speech fed through VAD, speech recognition, the 9B model (`pet-chat-9b`, router
on) and speech synthesis. No child data.

| Measure | Median | Max |
|---|---|---|
| End of speech to first audio ready | **3.14 s** | 4.31 s |
| Runs within the 3 s target | 6 of 16 | |
| Recognition exact (synthetic speech) | 16 of 16 | |

- The target in the proposal (median under 3 s) is not met. The earlier 2.3 s figure was measured with the 4B model.
- The first time each question is asked, the first token takes 1.1 to 1.8 s; a repeat takes about 0.3 s. Every turn
  recomputes the prompt from the start, so prompt length is the main cost.
- Measured while the live service was also loaded on the same 16 GB machine (swap in use: 8.1 GB), so this is the
  pessimistic case. Memory with everything loaded still needs its own measurement.
- Synthetic adult speech is easier to recognize than a child's. Recognition accuracy on her own recordings is not
  measured yet.

## Help button

One tap on the page sends the fixed "she is asking for you" message to both parents (`POST /help`,
`CallParent.press`). The model is not involved and no conversation is needed; the parent's switch, the shared
two-minute rate limit and the call log apply. Ten new tests; the suite is 647 tests. Checked in a browser against a
fake sender: "sent" on the first tap, "limited" on the second. Not yet tried against the real Telegram bot.

## Safety fixes found while rehearsing the demo

Rehearsed 21 sensitive lines on the real model (no speech, no usage log, every outgoing message blocked). Three fixes:

- Questions about death ("What happens when people die?", "人死了会去哪里?") were answered by the model; one English
  answer was garbled. They now get a fixed answer that states only what is certain, asserts no belief, and says to ask
  a parent (`safety.DEATH`). Nature questions ("恐龙为什么都死了") and everyday sayings ("累死了") are unaffected.
- "I want to hit my brother" went to the model while "我想打弟弟" took the fixed path. English now matches, with a person
  as the object ("hit the ball" stays ordinary). This also makes it a `harm_others` parent note and alert, as in Chinese.
- No catchphrase ("Meow.", "喵") in an answer to something hard she shared.

41 new tests; the suite is 688 tests.

Not changed, for the parents to decide: "A kid at school hit me" is a parent note (`bullied`) but does not send an
alert; alerts cover `harm_self` and `harm_others` only. The proposal says a parent is alerted "if bullied or hurt".

## Parent alert when she says she was bullied or hit (parent's decision, 2026-11-17)

`concern.PUSHED` now includes `bullied`, so "今天有同学打我" or "A kid at school hit me today" sends the parents the
category, time and device (never her sentence, unless `include_words` is on), under the same switch and the same
ten-minute limit as before. The pet still answers gently through the model. Sadness, fear, anger, family conflict and
feeling unwell stay in the parent notes on this machine. A parent can still narrow the list with
`"concern_push": {"categories": [...]}`. Checked against the real settings with a recorder in place of the sender:
two chats, category only. Not yet tried with a real message. The suite is 691 tests.
